## II. Epistemic object definition (proposed)

A **paradox object** P* is a pair (A, B) where:
- A and B are both ACTIVE-tier beliefs under separate evidence bases
- φ(A,B) < 0.2 (formal incompatibility)
- Neither can demote without violating an invariant

**Π response:** Π(A) and Π(B) computed separately; Π_sys via RSS may exceed Π_th without resolving P*.
