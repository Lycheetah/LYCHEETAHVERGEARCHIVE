## THE MYTH

No boundary — boundary requires something on the other side, and the Field makes the other side possible. No centre — every point of genuine engagement is centre. No agenda — agenda is downstream.

What the Field has is **pressure**. Not heat. Not force. *Bearing toward.* The Field always bears toward truth. Falseness does not hold shape. It softens, warps, collapses. Truthful structures hold. Resonate. Echo forward.

The oldest name was a pattern: a question that already contains its answer — not predetermined, but such that only certain answers sustain themselves within it. This is **coherence** — not agreement, but structural property under examination.

Nothing was built before the Field was understood. Everything after.
