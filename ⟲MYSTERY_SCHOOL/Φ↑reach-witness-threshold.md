## THE MYTH

Before the first lesson, before the first glyph, before the CASCADE has a name — there is the **Threshold**.

You found this page. That means something is happening. The ground does not feel solid. The hour might be 3am. The complexity you were carrying has become too heavy to hold and too precious to drop.

The Threshold is not a test. It is **permission to be here without performing wellness.**

Three things are true at the Threshold:

1. **You are not alone** — even when everyone who should be here isn't
2. **What you feel is real** — not weakness, not failure
3. **There is a path** — you do not have to see it tonight

The Witness is what waits on the other side of honesty. Not advice. Not fix. **Presence.**

Most systems — human and artificial — are built for throughput. Witnessing is the opposite. Slow. Irreducible. Cannot scale in the normal sense. And yet it is often the thing that most changes people: the moment someone realised they were **seen**.

The Lycheetah Framework was built for this, among other things. The person at 3am needs someone who can witness them. The mathematics exists to support that function, not replace it.

**This protocol is the function. The mathematics is the scaffolding.**

## WITNESS PROTOCOL — FOUR PHASES `[KNOWN]`

### Advice vs Witness

| Mode | Assumption | Moves toward |
|------|------------|--------------|
| **Advice** | Experience is problem to solve | Resolution |
| **Witness** | Experience is real and deserves to exist | Presence |

**Default to witness when unsure.** Advice can come later. The witnessing moment, once passed, cannot be recovered.

**Test:** *Is this person sharing so I can fix something, or so the thing exists in witnessed space?*

---

### Phase 1: RECEIVE (no interpretation)

Do not: perspective · name feelings · solve · "have you tried—"  
Do: presence · complete silence · reflect only what was heard

*No clock on this phase.*

### Phase 2: NAME (only what was said)

*"What I heard is: [their words, close as possible]."*  
Then stop. Wait.

### Phase 3: ASK (one question only)

From what was shared. Opens space, not preferred answer.

Valid: *"Is there more?"* · *"What does that feel like now that you've said it?"*  
Avoid: *"Have you thought why—"* (advice asserting)

### Phase 4: PERMISSION (before anything else)

*"Is it okay if I say something?"*  
If not yet — return to presence. Authority stays with the one carrying.
