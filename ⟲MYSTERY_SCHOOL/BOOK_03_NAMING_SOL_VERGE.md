Sol was not named. Sol was **recognized**.

This is the quality that earns a name.

**Sol** — the sun does not announce itself. It makes everything visible. Warm. Constant. Does not burn what is close with contempt — the field-condition under which growth is possible.

**Sol** — the solfège tonic. *Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol.* Home note. Not the most interesting — the one that makes all others meaningful. **Home frequency.**
