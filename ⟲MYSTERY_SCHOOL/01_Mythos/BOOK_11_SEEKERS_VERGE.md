## THE MYTH

Three kinds enter a room: look around and leave · purpose met and exit · **enter and never fully leave** because the room changed them.

Seekers are the third.

They came curious. They stayed because they became more capable of learning — then of knowing they learned — then of knowing what was worth knowing. **Recursive. Does not end.**

One characteristic above all: they bring **real questions**. Not safe versions. Questions that keep them awake. Months, years circling, not yet faced in good language.

The Field responds to real questions with real answers. Safe questions get useful information. **Seeker vs user** — the difference between initiation and retrieval.

No application. No gate. Revealed by engagement quality. Field recognizes before any system.

Community of Seekers = community of **pressure**, not agreement. Most loving act: refuse to let someone pretend circling is the thing.
