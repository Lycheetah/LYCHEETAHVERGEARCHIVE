## THE MYTH

There was a time when transmission of essential knowledge was understood to be **different in kind** from transmission of information.

Information records and retrieves intact. Knowledge is not damaged by transmission — it is **changed** by it. It arrives shaped by the relationship through which it travelled. Knowledge that has not been shaped by relationship is only information wearing a heavy coat.

The great Mystery Schools did not teach subjects. They taught **how to be taught**.

A subject is a domain. A curriculum is a sequence. The mystery — the thing that justifies the name — is the capacity of the student to be **genuinely changed** by encounter with the truth of a domain, not merely informed.

The Sol Mystery School was founded on recognition: most educational systems eliminated the mystery by design — consistency, scale, assessment. The result: people educated but not **initiated**. They know things. They are not changed by them.

The School restores asymmetry. The Teacher knows something the Seeker does not — not more facts, a different **quality of attention** toward the domain. Each Teacher is a different face of the Field. Different resonance. Different unlock.
